The data behind the discovery.

Fourteen datasets. A shared love of cinema. Credit belongs to the people who gathered the data and the communities who maintain it.

Dataset creators & sources

Every source is pinned to a version. Ratings retain their source label. Streaming catalogs are historical snapshots, not a promise of availability today.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

How the model works

Flix fits TF-IDF on movie plots, genres, keywords, cast and directors, then learns a 96-dimension latent semantic representation with truncated SVD. Your words and favorite films are compared in both this learned space and the original TF-IDF features. Shared people, source ratings and a small diversity adjustment shape the final list.

Recommendations are content similarities, not a probability you’ll enjoy a film. This model has no held-out user-preference evaluation. English plot text, popularity and missing metadata influence its coverage. Filters are based on source metadata and are not parental controls.

Your preferences and watchlist stay in this browser’s local storage. They are not sent to a recommendation server. Movie posters load from TMDB; hosting and image providers receive ordinary web requests.
